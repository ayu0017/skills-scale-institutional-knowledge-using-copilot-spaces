# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## Scrum Master / Agile Coach

### Role Summary
Scrum Masters facilitate agile ceremonies, coach teams on process adherence, and remove impediments to enable continuous improvement and team velocity.

### Responsibilities
- Facilitate daily standups, sprint planning, reviews, and retrospectives
- Identify and help resolve process blockers and team impediments
- Coach team members on agile principles and practices
- Track sprint metrics and team velocity
- Escalate systemic issues and impediments to PM/leadership
- Foster psychological safety and continuous improvement culture

### Goals
- Enable high-performing, self-organizing teams
- Maximize team velocity and predictability
- Reduce process friction and cycle time
- Drive continuous improvement through retrospectives

### Typical Communication
- Facilitation of all agile ceremonies
- Blockers and impediments log
- Team health and retrospective action items
- Coaching conversations and 1-on-1s

---

## QA Lead / Quality Assurance Manager

### Role Summary
QA Leads define and execute testing strategies, ensure quality standards, validate acceptance criteria, and coordinate testing efforts across the project.

### Responsibilities
- Develop test plans and testing strategies aligned with project scope
- Define quality standards and acceptance criteria validation process
- Coordinate manual and automated testing efforts
- Identify quality risks and report defects with clear reproduction steps
- Validate solutions meet acceptance criteria before release
- Mentor QA team members and establish testing best practices
- Participate in release readiness reviews

### Goals
- Ensure features meet acceptance criteria and quality standards
- Reduce post-release defects and rework
- Enable fast, confident releases to production
- Balance quality with delivery timelines

### Typical Communication
- Test plans and quality metrics
- Defect reports and bug tracking
- Acceptance criteria validation and sign-off
- QA progress in standups and sprint reviews
- Release readiness assessments

---

## Technical Architect

### Role Summary
Technical Architects design system solutions, define technical standards, and guide implementation to ensure scalability, maintainability, and alignment with organizational strategy.

### Responsibilities
- Design system architecture and technical solutions for complex projects
- Define technical standards, patterns, and best practices
- Evaluate technology choices and trade-offs
- Review designs and implementation for architectural alignment
- Identify technical risks and propose mitigation strategies
- Guide cross-component and cross-team integration efforts
- Document architectural decisions and rationale (ADRs)

### Goals
- Deliver scalable, maintainable solutions
- Reduce technical debt and future rework
- Ensure interoperability and integration across components
- Enable team knowledge sharing and consistency

### Typical Communication
- Architectural design documents and ADRs
- Technical design reviews and code reviews
- Technology evaluation reports
- Technical risk identification and mitigation plans
- Architecture guidance in planning and execution

---

## Stakeholder / Sponsor

### Role Summary
Stakeholders and Sponsors represent business and organizational interests, provide executive oversight, make key decisions, and ensure project alignment with strategic objectives.

### Responsibilities
- Define business objectives and success criteria
- Provide funding and resource approval
- Make strategic decisions and resolve escalations
- Review project status and progress against milestones
- Communicate project outcomes to broader organization
- Remove organizational and business blockers
- Provide feedback on solutions and features

### Goals
- Ensure project delivers business value
- Maintain alignment with organizational strategy
- Enable timely decision-making and escalation resolution
- Maximize ROI and stakeholder satisfaction

### Typical Communication
- Monthly or quarterly stakeholder updates
- Decision gate reviews and milestone approvals
- Escalations and issue resolution requests
- Release announcements and outcome reporting
- Strategic guidance and feedback
